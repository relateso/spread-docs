## Overview

Spread | Emma is an AI marketing agent that automatically sends emails from the moment a potential customer's email address is captured, reads their responses, and converts them into sales opportunities.

It automates lead nurturing through email, determines the optimal sending timing based on the recipient’s actual responses, and has an AI Writer that drafts context-appropriate emails on your behalf.
  
As a result, regardless of the acquisition channel, it creates a flow that never misses the moment of engagement and naturally leads to meetings.


---
## Use Cases Where Spread | Emma Is Needed

### 1. When inbound inquiries do not convert into meetings  

When a demo request is submitted through the website, an automated response email is sent immediately.
However, due to the lack of clear criteria on when and how to follow up afterward,
the optimal timing to convert the inquiry into a meeting is often missed.

**By using Spread | Emma, you can automatically determine whether to propose a meeting immediately or continue additional communication, based on email responses after an inbound inquiry.**

<div style={{ height: "5px" }} />
### 2. When it is difficult to decide the next action after content downloads    

Even if a lead downloads a guide or resource, it is difficult to determine which content they were actually interested in or whether they merely viewed it.
As a result, leads are often left unattended without any follow-up communication.

**Spread | Emma tracks content views and click responses, and can automatically send appropriate follow-up content or messages based on the lead’s level of interest.**

<div style={{ height: "5px" }} />
### 3. When leads are left unattended after webinars / events    

Many leads participate in webinars or events, but communication often ends with a single thank-you email after the event.
Who actively participated, who asked questions, and who is willing to continue the conversation are not clearly organized.

**By using Spread | Emma,
you can segment leads based on engagement and responses,
and prioritize follow-up communication starting with the most interested leads.**


<div style={{ height: "5px" }} />
### 4. When you cannot leverage responses after outbound emails    
When outbound emails (cold emails) are sent, some leads open the email or click links.
However, if these responses are not connected to the next action,
email engagement remains only as a metric.

**Spread | Emma
uses email responses as signals
to automatically continue follow-up messages or meeting proposals.**


<div style={{ height: "5px" }} />
### 5. When you fail to drive usage after free trials / sign-ups    

When a customer starts a free trial or completes sign-up, a welcome email is sent.
However, it is often unclear which features should be introduced first,
or who needs help and when.

**Spread | Emma
automatically sends the necessary guidance messages based on users’ actual behavior, 
helping trials and early usage continue naturally.**


<div style={{ height: "5px" }} />
### 6. When inactive existing leads do not re-engage    
Among leads that were contacted in the past,
many conversations were paused due to poor timing or lack of response.
These leads often remain without further management.

**By leveraging Spread | Emma,
you can resend low-pressure content to existing leads,
and connect only responsive leads back into the sales flow.**

## Changes After Adopting Spread | Emma

### **1. More leads are converted into real sales opportunities**

Previously, after sending emails, lead management often did not continue,
and even responsive leads were not connected to appropriate follow-up actions.

After adopting Spread | Emma,
next actions are automatically triggered based on email responses.
	- Leads with confirmed interest are immediately connected to meeting proposals.
	- Leads that are not yet ready go through nurturing and are later converted back into sales opportunities.

As a result, email lists no longer function as simple sending targets,
but become an active part of the actual sales pipeline.

### **2. Previously discarded leads are reused**

Leads that were not managed simply because they did not respond once
can be systematically managed again through Spread | Emma.

Campaigns are used to re-confirm interest, and once a response occurs,
the lead is naturally transitioned back into the sales flow.

In other words, new sales opportunities can be created again
from leads you already had.

### **3. You no longer miss the right timing**

The moment a lead opens, clicks, or replies to an email
is when their interest is most active.

Spread | Emma detects these responses in real time
and automatically executes the next action before a human needs to check.

With follow-up communication continuing at the right timing,
meetings are no longer accidental, but structurally created.


### **4. It runs automatically even when the company is busy**

Even when you are busy with meetings, schedules, or other tasks,
Emma’s automation flows do not stop.
- Leads are continuously segmented,
- Emails are sent based on configured conditions, and
- Lead responses are connected back into the next flow.

Regardless of the team’s situation,
sales opportunities continue to accumulate.
